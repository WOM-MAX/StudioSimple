## 6. Explanation and model

**Concepto:** Explicit information is stated directly. A question word tells you what to look for: when asks about time, who asks about a person or character, and where asks about a place. Keep the diary date separate from the event date.

**The question word:** Read the question word first. When directs us to a date or time in the resource.

**Two dates:** The heading dates the entry on June thirteen. The birthday described took place on June twelve.

**Two morning times:** Anne woke at six but waited until six forty-five to get up. These are different actions.

**Who welcomed Anne?:** The resource names Moortje as the cat who welcomed Anne in the dining room.

**The evidence:** Return to the line that contains the requested information. Answer the actual question instead of another similar one.

**Modelo explicado:** The birthday was on Friday, 12 June. The entry was dated Saturday, 13 June 1942. The heading and the first event sentence provide different dates.

**Cómo se construye el modelo, mostrar durante la enseñanza:** 1) Leer la pregunta y precisar qué pide. 2) Localizar en el caso: «Heading: Saturday, 13 June 1942. Birthday: Friday, 12 June.». 3) Explicar la relación: The heading and the first event sentence provide different dates. 4) Formular la respuesta: The birthday was on Friday, 12 June. The entry was dated Saturday, 13 June 1942. Comprobar que cada afirmación conserva los datos del recurso.

**Error frecuente:** Answering with the heading date when the question asks for the birthday date.

**Comprobación inmediata:** Did she wake up and get up at the same time?

**Modelo de comprobación:** No. She woke up at 6:00 and got up at 6:45.
