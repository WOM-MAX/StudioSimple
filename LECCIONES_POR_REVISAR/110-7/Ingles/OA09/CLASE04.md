## 6. Explanation and model

**Concepto:** The main idea covers the purpose of the whole email. Explicit details answer questions about its meeting, materials and activities. An opinion about the email should use a detail from it.

**The main idea:** The email explains a reading club meeting and what to do there. The main idea covers that whole purpose.

**When and where:** The meeting is on Wednesday at three o'clock. Its place is the school library.

**The materials:** Mia asks Leo to bring a notebook and a pencil. These are explicit details.

**The activity:** The club will read a short story about friendship and discuss different ideas respectfully.

**A supported opinion:** Mia thinks sharing ideas helps understanding. A reader can agree or disagree and refer to the discussion described.

**Modelo explicado:** It is mainly about a reading club meeting and how to take part. The details all concern preparing for and joining the meeting.

**Cómo se construye el modelo, mostrar durante la enseñanza:** 1) Leer la pregunta y precisar qué pide. 2) Localizar en el caso: «The email includes the meeting time, place, materials and activity.». 3) Explicar la relación: The details all concern preparing for and joining the meeting. 4) Formular la respuesta: It is mainly about a reading club meeting and how to take part. Comprobar que cada afirmación conserva los datos del recurso.

**Error frecuente:** Choosing one material as the main idea ignores the purpose of the whole email.

**Comprobación inmediata:** When and where does the club meet?

**Modelo de comprobación:** It meets on Wednesday at three o'clock in the school library.
