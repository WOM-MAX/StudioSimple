## 3. Starting point

### Student reading resource

Original classroom text for this lesson, not a textbook quotation.

Our school garden
Our class is starting a small school garden. On Monday, we prepared the soil. On Tuesday, we planted seeds. We check the soil before adding water. We keep a notebook to record what we observe. The plants do not all grow at the same speed. Next week, we will compare our observations. Our teacher says that caring for the garden is a team task. I agree because each person helps with a different job.

Vocabulary: soil = ground where plants grow; seeds = parts from which new plants can grow; observations = things noticed and recorded.

**Before reading:** What do you expect from the title? Give a prediction, then check it by reading.

**Punto de partida / Starting point:** Which actions are completed and which is a plan?

## 6. Explanation and model

**Concepto:** A sequence tells the order of events. Day names and expressions such as next week help readers distinguish past actions from plans. The main idea covers the whole project, not one day only.

**The main idea:** The text is about a school garden project. Preparing, planting and recording are parts of that project.

**The first action:** On Monday, the class prepared the soil. The day helps us place the action in the sequence.

**The next action:** On Tuesday, the class planted seeds. This action follows preparing the soil.

**Observations and plans:** The class keeps a notebook and plans to compare observations next week. A plan is different from a completed event.

**A reasoned opinion:** The writer agrees that garden care is a team task because people help with different jobs.

**Modelo explicado:** Preparing the soil and planting seeds are completed. Comparing observations next week is a plan. The day names and the expression next week mark different times.

**Cómo se construye el modelo, mostrar durante la enseñanza:** 1) Leer la pregunta y precisar qué pide. 2) Localizar en el caso: «Monday: prepared the soil. Tuesday: planted seeds. Next week: compare observations.». 3) Explicar la relación: The day names and the expression next week mark different times. 4) Formular la respuesta: Preparing the soil and planting seeds are completed. Comparing observations next week is a plan. Comprobar que cada afirmación conserva los datos del recurso.

**Error frecuente:** Treating a plan for next week as an event that has already happened.

**Comprobación inmediata:** How does the writer support the opinion about teamwork?

**Modelo de comprobación:** The writer agrees because different people help with different jobs.
